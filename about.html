About the maison

We watch how people carry.

Carried is an independent website about bags and the way we carry them.

I.

We write about carry styles, straps, weight and posture, so the bags we love are also comfortable to live with.

II.

Our carry studio and bag weigher let you see and test the ideas for yourself.

III.

We do not sell bags, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
