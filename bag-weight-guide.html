Guide II

Bag Weight & Posture

A heavy bag on one shoulder, every day, adds up. A few simple habits keep the load kind to your neck, shoulders and back.

  1. I

    How heavy is too heavy

    A common guideline is to keep a shoulder bag under about 10 percent of your body weight, and lighter for everyday use. If you lean to one side, feel a strap digging in or ache at the end of the day, the bag is too heavy.

  2. II

    Lighten the load

    • Empty the bag once a week.
    • Carry only what you need that day.
    • Swap a heavy wallet for a slim card holder.
    • Use a pouch to move essentials between bags.
  3. III

    Spread the weight

    Wide straps spread pressure across the shoulder. Crossbody straps and backpacks share it across the body. Pack heavy items at the bottom and close to your body so the bag does not pull you backwards.

  4. IV

    Switch and stretch

    Change shoulders or hands every so often during the day. Roll your shoulders back and down when you put the bag down, and stand tall rather than hitching one shoulder to stop a strap slipping.

  5. V

    Choose the right bag for the day

    Light days suit hand-held and shoulder bags. Days with a laptop, shopping or a long walk call for a crossbody or a backpack. Matching the bag to the load is the simplest posture habit of all.
