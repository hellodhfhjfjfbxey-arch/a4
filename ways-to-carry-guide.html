Ways to Carry a Bag

Every bag has a natural way to be carried. Choose the right one and the bag looks better, lasts longer and feels lighter.

  1. I

    In the hand

    Top-handle bags are made to be held by the handles, arm relaxed at your side. It looks polished and formal, but the weight sits on your fingers and wrist, so keep the load light and switch hands often.

  2. II

    In the crook of the arm

    Resting the handles in the bend of the elbow, forearm raised, is elegant and keeps the bag in view. It suits small, structured bags. It tires the arm quickly, so lower it whenever you can.

  3. III

    On the shoulder

    A shoulder strap or long handles let the bag sit under the arm or at the hip. It frees the hands and suits medium bags. Swap shoulders regularly so one side does not carry all the load.

  4. IV

    Across the body

    A crossbody strap spreads weight across the torso and keeps hands free and the bag secure in crowds.

    • Wear the bag at hip height.
    • Adjust the strap so the bag does not swing.
    • Choose a wider strap for heavier loads.
  5. V

    On the back

    Backpacks with two straps share weight evenly between both shoulders. They are the kindest option for heavy loads. Use both straps, tighten them so the bag sits high and close, and keep the heaviest items nearest your back.
